Two models, loaded at the same time and chosen independently. Role model (masked checkpoint): each team's five roles in TOP→UTILITY order, picked champions placed in their most likely role — the champion you just picked always keeps the role the model likes best for it — and the model's target for every empty role (top-3 from the role-conditioned masked queries). Next move (policy checkpoint): one ranked list for the side on the clock, plus ban candidates ranked by how likely the opponent was to take them. Fearless is an input to the next-move model and is masked on picks only, so a champion picked in an earlier game can still be banned; the role model has no such input, so there its answers are filtered, not conditioned.
